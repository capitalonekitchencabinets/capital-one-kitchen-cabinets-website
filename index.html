How We Build

Inside your cabinets: the plywood box, explained

A cabinet is only as good as the box behind the door. Here is what goes into every Capital One kitchen.

1

The Plywood Box

Cabinet boxes built from plywood — stronger and more moisture-resistant than particleboard, so doors stay aligned and shelves don’t sag.

2

Doors & Fronts

Acrylic, melamine, oak, maple or painted MDF doors — finished smooth and matched across every door, drawer and panel.

3

Soft-Close Hinges

Quality soft-close hinges fitted as standard — no slamming, no worn corners.

4

Soft-Close Drawer Runners

Full-extension soft-close runners, so drawers open fully and close quietly every time.

5

Adjustable Shelves

Shelves you can move as your storage changes — in kitchens, pantries, vanities and wardrobes.

6

Measured & Made to Fit

Every run is measured on site, so fillers are minimal and the fit is clean in your exact space.

Plywood Box — What We Build

  • Holds screws tight — doors stay aligned
  • Handles moisture — better near sinks & dishwashers
  • Shelves don’t sag under heavy loads
  • Built to last for decades of daily use

Particleboard / Chipboard Box — The Cheap Alternative

  • Screws loosen as the board crumbles
  • Swells and bubbles when it gets wet
  • Shelves sag under weight
  • Cheaper today — replaced sooner
